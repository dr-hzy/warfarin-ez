Warfarin Initiation Phase Protocol (Day 1 – 14)

Clinical Reminder: The main Warfarin-EZ calculator is designed strictly for the Maintenance Phase. For patients starting warfarin therapy, adhere to the MOH Appendix 11 (Table 1 & Table 2) algorithm below:

Patient Cohort Initial Starting Dose (Day 1 - 3) Day 8 INR Assessment & Titration
Non-Sensitive (Standard) 5 mg / 5 mg, then 3 mg OD • INR < 1.5: Increase weekly dose by 10%–15%, TCA 7 days
• INR 1.5–1.9: Maintain dose, TCA 7–14 days
• INR 2.0–3.0: Reduce 0.5–1 mg from weekly dose, TCA 7–10 days
• INR > 3.0: Hold and reinitiate at lower dose when INR in range, TCA 7 days
Sensitive Patients (Elderly ≥65 yrs, malnourished, CHF, liver disease, amiodarone) 3 mg, then 2 mg OD Titrate based on Day 8 INR response as above with closer monitoring (every 3–7 days).
Key Principles of Warfarin Initiation:
1. Do NOT administer loading doses ≥10 mg (increased risk of skin necrosis and paradoxical hypercoagulation).
2. Obtain baseline PT/INR, LFT, and FBC before the first dose.
3. When rapid anticoagulation is required (e.g., acute DVT/PE), overlap with therapeutic LMWH for at least 5 days and until INR is therapeutic for 2 consecutive days.

Warfarin Reversal Protocol & Referral Criteria (MOH App 19)

KLINIK KESIHATAN REFERRAL CUT-OFF: Any INR > 5.0 or any patient presenting with clinical bleeding / red flag symptoms MUST be discussed with the Family Medicine Specialist (FMS) or referred to the emergency department / referral hospital.
INR Level Clinical Setting Management Recommendation
< 5.0 No bleeding Omit 1 dose tonight and lower weekly dose by 10%–15% (or resume prior dose if spike was due to a single accidental double dose). TCA in 7 days. Routine Vitamin K is NOT required.
5.0 – 8.9 No bleeding Hold 1–2 doses of warfarin. Reduce weekly dose by 15%. Recheck INR in 3–5 days.
Oral Vitamin K (1–2.5 mg) is only considered if the patient has a high bleeding risk (HAS-BLED ≥3). Consult FMS.
> 9.0 No bleeding Hold warfarin. Administer oral Vitamin K 2.5 mg – 5 mg. Repeat INR in 24–48 hours. Consult FMS / refer to Hospital if unstable.
Any INR Serious / Major Bleeding URGENT HOSPITAL EMERGENCY REFERRAL: Stop warfarin immediately. Administer Vitamin K 10 mg (slow IV infusion) + 4-Factor PCC or FFP per hospital protocol. Resuscitate with fluids/blood as needed.

Clinical Assessment Guide: Causes of ↑ INR vs. ↓ INR (MOH AC-MTAC)

Category Factors That INCREASE INR (↑ Bleeding Risk) Factors That REDUCE INR (↑ Clotting Risk)

• Antiarrhythmics: Amiodarone
• Analgesics/Antiplatelets: NSAIDs (ibuprofen, naproxen) & aspirin (increase bleeding risk)

Key Clinical Takeaways for MO Consultation:
• 3-Day Recall Rule: Patients initiated on interacting antibiotics (metronidazole, ciprofloxacin, Bactrim, fluconazole) or amiodarone should return in 3 days to recheck INR.
• Lab Error Check: If an INR deviates unexpectedly without clinical explanation, repeat the INR same-day or next-day before adjusting maintenance doses.
